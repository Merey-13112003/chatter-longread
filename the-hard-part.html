Neila's voice note

Neila on the difficulties she faced when trying to find friends

[When we were children, friendship happened by itself. You shared a desk, a playground or a summer, and that was enough. Adult life has no such shortcuts. Our days are split between work, family and the road home, and old friends drift away into other cities, other schedules, other lives. We are not lonely because nobody is around. We are lonely because there is rarely a reason to start talking.]

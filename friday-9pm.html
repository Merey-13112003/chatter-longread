Epilogue

Friday, 9 PM

The meeting officially ended an hour ago. The tablet has finally gone dark, the cups are empty, and the topic of the day is long forgotten. But nobody is in a hurry to leave.

Now they talk about work, about weekends, about where to go next Friday. Someone is adding someone else on Telegram. Someone is laughing at a joke that only makes sense if you were here last week.

For an adult, asking a stranger to be your friend feels almost impossible. Saying "let's talk" turns out to be much easier. And sometimes, that is exactly how a friendship starts.

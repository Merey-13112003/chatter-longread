Let's Talk.

Chapter 1

Friday, 7 PM

Friday evening. The coffee shop is filled with rolling laughter one moment and thoughtful silence the next. The presentation on a tablet keeps fading out, and on its screen, in capital letters, is the topic for tonight's discussion.

Among the ceramic cups of tea and coffee stands a soft pink sign with the name of the speaking club: Chatterbox. People have come here for different reasons, but all of them want to practise speaking English. For some, it is a chance to improve their speaking skills. For others, it is a chance to improve their social ones.

At the centre of the table, the club's founder, Ainar, usually greets the newcomers. She tries to welcome every new member herself and to give them attention and warmth. After all, being the new one in a group is never easy.

A girl at the end of the table starts a sentence in English, stumbles, and laughs at herself. Two people suggest the word at once, one from each side. A minute later, she is already arguing.

Let's Talk.

Chapter 7

From Strangers to Friends

Ainar on how club members started becoming friends

[It rarely happens in one evening. First, you recognise faces. Then you remember names. Then someone suggests a coffee after the club, a walk, a picnic in the park, a birthday party, a Halloween night. The photos on everyone’s phones slowly change: fewer pictures of slides and tables, more pictures of people together. And one day you notice that the people you met over a discussion topic are the ones you call first.]
